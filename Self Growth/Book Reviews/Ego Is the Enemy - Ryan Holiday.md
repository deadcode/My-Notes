# The insights from Ego Is the Enemy that actually stuck with me

![r/Growthmindsetbookclub - The insights from Ego Is the Enemy that actually stuck with me](https://preview.redd.it/the-insights-from-ego-is-the-enemy-that-actually-stuck-with-v0-q3djcsycb9kh1.jpeg?width=640&crop=smart&auto=webp&s=e22bb45788a42ea53bde7714ac5f5ef5face9ed6)

Read this expecting motivation. Instead got a mirror showing me how often I sabotage myself without realizing it.

Ego makes you talk instead of work.

Holiday points out that talking about your goals gives you a dopamine hit similar to actually achieving them. Your brain can't tell the difference. So you announce the project, feel good, then lose the drive to execute. People with ego problems are always talking about what they're going to do. People with results are too busy doing it to talk.

Ego makes you fragile when you should be learning.

When someone criticizes your work, ego hears an attack on your identity. It gets defensive, dismissive, or wounded. The opportunity to improve disappears because you're too busy protecting your self-image. Holiday argues that the ability to receive feedback without emotional reaction is one of the biggest advantages you can develop.

Ego hits hardest after early success.

The most dangerous moment is right after your first win. Ego tells you that you've figured it out. You stop listening to advice. You assume past methods will keep working. You surround yourself with people who agree with you. Holiday documents how many successful people flame out not because they lost talent, but because ego made them unteachable.

The antidote is staying a student permanently.

Holiday's solution isn't false humility. It's genuine curiosity. The moment you believe you've arrived is the moment you stop improving. Every master still considers themselves a student. Every expert knows how much they don't know. Ego closes that awareness off.

The uncomfortable realization from this book is that ego operates invisibly. You don't notice it running the show. You just notice the consequences later.

What's a time ego got in your way without you realizing it until after?
